# Embodiment without a second semantic owner

Status: **IMPLEMENTED REALITY** for existing Runtime presentation/voice/vision and Desktop/Supervisor assets; **PRODUCTION ARCHITECTURE DECISION** for ownership; robot extensions are **RESEARCH HYPOTHESIS** / later engineering.

The old Life Layer bundles unrelated jobs and is retired. Runtime owns execution, prospective scheduling and permission; context selection owns evidence choice; client presentation owns rendering, playback and local interaction. Character chooses authorized expression. None gains a second journal, agenda, identity writer or meaning-making loop.

Preserve existing Runtime embodied-effect admission/fencing/outcome acceptance, turn replacement, voice barge-in, subtitle/Live2D presentation and visual-grounding privacy. Desktop/Supervisor retain owned-process start, seal/drain, bounded stop and force-stop responsibilities. Window close/hide behavior is not a semantic life state. A9 records intent and outcome evidence at these existing boundaries, rather than redesigning presentation.

A playback-start report means the device reported starting, not that a Person listened. A gesture completion report means the client reports completion, not that an emotion was experienced. Screenshots and ambient audio are observations with consent/retention restrictions, not unrestricted durable Memory. Missing reports remain unknown.

Future robots require local real-time actuator safety, collision/force limits, watchdogs and fail-safe control independent of cloud/LLM latency. Runtime may issue bounded goals through certified capabilities; local controllers can refuse or stop them. Journal attempts/outcomes retain uncertainty and do not replace physical safety. A “cerebellum” metaphor adds no implementation authority; name the actual controller and contract. Robot work, broad animation/UI redesign and active exploration are outside v0.1.3.
